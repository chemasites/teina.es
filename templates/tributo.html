Indie pop rock tribute band in Murcia, Spain

Teína is an indie pop rock tribute band based in Calasparra, in the Northwest of the Region of Murcia. We bring the best songs of Spanish and international indie to the live stage, reinterpreted with our own identity, alongside original tracks. If you're looking for an indie pop rock tribute band in Murcia for your concert, festival, town celebration, wedding or private event, we connect with the audience from the first chord.
